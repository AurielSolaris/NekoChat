<p><b>Private AI that runs on your phone.</b></p>

<p>NekoChat chats with language models running entirely on your device, through its own C++ inference engine. Your conversations never leave the phone, and there are no accounts, ads or analytics. The internet is only used when you choose to download a model.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Models:</b> GPT-2, Qwen3 (0.6B and up) and IBM Granite 4.0-H (350M, 1B), from .safetensors or PyTorch checkpoints. Outputs match the Hugging Face reference token for token</li>
<li><b>Your own engine:</b> runs on Vulkan, OpenGL ES 3.1 or the CPU (NEON). A GPU is only used after it passes a self-test</li>
<li><b>Weight precision:</b> FP16, FP8 or FP4, quantized while the model loads. FP8 halves the memory with nearly the same answers</li>
<li><b>Downloads:</b> one-tap models from Hugging Face, or any public repository. Resumes where it stopped. Or just pick a folder of models you already have</li>
<li><b>Chats:</b> as many as you like, each with its own temperature, top-k, top-p, repetition penalty, reply length, seed and persona. Redo a reply or edit your message</li>
<li><b>Voice input:</b> tap the mic and talk. Granite Speech turns it into text on the phone (English, downloaded on request); the recording never leaves the device</li>
<li><b>Read aloud:</b> replies and actions spoken by Kokoro-82M, a text-to-speech model that also runs on the phone (downloaded on request), or by the phone's own voice</li>
<li><b>Memory indicator:</b> see what the model, chat memory and the app use</li>
<li><b>Themes:</b> Rose, Ocean and Gothic Pink (built to WCAG 2.2 AA contrast)</li>
</ul>

<p>Models are not included; NekoChat downloads them from Hugging Face when you ask, or reads them from a folder you choose. Small models (350M to 1B parameters) run comfortably on phones with 4 GB of RAM or more.</p>
